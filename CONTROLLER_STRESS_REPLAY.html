Design change supported by a failure

V1 rejected 0/9 noisy-blur challenge inputs. Noise inflated the edge score. V2 adds a reference-aware fine-residual guard and rejected 9/9 on the reused development benchmark. The separate follow-up changes timestamps and some perturbation settings, but is not an independent physical experiment.

Decision rules

Fail if mean grayscale is below 20 or above 235/255, if more than 20% of pixels are near white, if brightness-normalized edge strength falls below 55% of its reference, or if fine residuals exceed the larger of 3 levels and 2.5 times the reference residual. These are provisional engineering settings. Fine residuals can also rise with real texture changes.

The retry policy escalates after three failed attempts or the elapsed budget. The callback must have its own timeout because the policy cannot interrupt a blocked hardware call. There is no automatic reference replacement, focus movement or service restart.
