This is a proposed integration contract for discussion with DSC. No lab storage credentials, camera connection or deployed recapture endpoint are included in the public demo.

Available now

A shared, self-guided demo with current prototype examples, separate archival organoid examples, local image trials and downloadable numerical measurements. Local trials stay in the visitor's browser; they do not create a shared dataset.

Proposed live connection

StepResponsibility
Capture imageExisting scanner supplies fresh image, capture ID, well, timestamp, settings and reference ID.
EvaluateMariam's module returns measured features, reasons and ACCEPT, RETRY or ESCALATE; missing references remain unresolved.
Request recaptureExisting scanner executes an authorized fresh capture within retry/time limits; the quality module does not directly move motors.
Store outcomeExisting storage records original images, attempts, quality decisions, parameters and timestamps together.

Discuss with DSC

  • Agree where the gate belongs in the existing capture-to-storage workflow.
  • Choose the image endpoint or storage-event interface; preserve existing storage and ownership.
  • Define reference selection, acquisition settings and timeout behavior.
  • Test in an individual branch, review results, then merge and deploy together.
  • Choose authenticated upload permissions before enabling persistent shared uploads.

Validation still needed

Finish human quality labels and reference verification; measure agreement and unresolved coverage. Validate physical recaptures and settling delays on hardware, then test prospectively on next-quarter organoids. Archival images do not establish current-prototype organoid performance.
